# Customer Data and Privacy

Applies to: support agents and the support assistant

## Personal data

Customer names, email addresses and phone numbers are personal data. Rider names and phone numbers are personal data too.

## What support may share

Support may confirm an order's status, times and compensation to the customer who placed it. Support never shares one person's contact details with anyone else.

## Masking

Outside the support admin group, email and phone are shown masked: four stars followed by the last 4 characters.

## Promises to customers

Never promise a coupon or a refund before the four facts have been checked against these SOPs.
