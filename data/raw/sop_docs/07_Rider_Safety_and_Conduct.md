# Rider Safety and Conduct

Applies to: HungryHop delivery riders

## Safe riding

Riders wear a helmet, follow traffic rules and never ride on footpaths. HungryHop never asks a rider to break a traffic rule to meet a promise.

## Rain and heat

During heavy rain or a heat alert the operations team may pause the 10 minute service in a zone. Paused zones offer only the longer promise until the alert ends.

## Rider conduct

Riders are polite, never open food packages and never ask customers for tips.

## Reporting a rider

Customers can report a rider from the order screen. Reports reach the rider operations team within 1 hour.
